WormAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Developer: sohomc87

WormAI is a free, single-player, offline arcade game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and what happens to it. In short: WormAI does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. WormAI has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send information anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To work as a game, WormAI saves a few things only on your own phone: your settings (such as sound, music, controls and vibration), your player name, scores, leaderboard and progress through the game's levels. This information never leaves your device through WormAI. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

If you have turned on Android's own backup in your phone settings, Android may include this game data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account and Google's privacy policy. You can manage this in your phone's backup settings.

Permissions

WormAI uses only the Vibrate permission, so the phone can buzz briefly when you eat, hit a wall or are eliminated. You can switch vibration off in the game's settings.

Google Play features

WormAI is distributed through Google Play. Two Google Play features run inside the Google Play Store app rather than inside WormAI:

  • Ratings and reviews. Occasionally, after a win or a new best score, the game may ask Google Play to show its standard rating window. Any rating or review you choose to leave is sent by the Google Play Store app directly to Google. WormAI never sees or receives it.
  • Install check. Google Play may confirm that the game was installed from Google Play.

These features are governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms.

Links

If you tap a link in the game (for example to the WormAI page on Google Play or to this policy), it opens in the Play Store app or your web browser. Those apps and websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

WormAI is designed for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
